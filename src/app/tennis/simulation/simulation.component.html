Every eligible wager in the full stored prediction/odds overlap is included in the calculation. The line retains the exact end-of-day bankroll state in chronological order, avoiding redundant intraday pixels while preserving the full first-to-last date range and ending result. Both player sides are evaluated from complementary model probabilities; only a positive raw-EV side meeting the selected edge threshold enters these curves. Kelly and bounded staking start from a 1,000 bankroll; all three charts show cumulative profit so each begins at zero. Results before the registered out-of-sample boundary are descriptive in-sample/calibration history, not unbiased evidence of future performance. Long-horizon percentage and Kelly compounding are mathematical stress scenarios; their exponential totals are not a realistic capital forecast.
