# Court backgrounds

Profile → Display controls the palette and backdrop independently. The default follows the original stadium for each palette. Backgrounds are decorative, stored only in the current browser, and omitted when printing or using increased contrast. The tennis net is drawn in CSS.

The two photographic scenes were generated for BonzoByte using OpenAI image generation. Web-optimised exports and picker thumbnails are in `src/assets/backgrounds/`.

## night-serve

Assets: `night-serve-v1.webp`, `night-serve-thumb-v1.webp`.

Prompt: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Single edge-to-edge horizontal 16:9 wide photograph, not a mockup. This is a premium understated background for a tennis stats site whose large table hides the middle 65% of the image. Realistic regulation tennis court and net, natural anatomy, no logos, text, UI, sponsor boards, brands or recognizable people. Detailed enough to reward looking at the side margins, gentle contrast and continuous natural composition, not blacked out in the middle. Blue hour on an indigo hard court at a small floodlit arena. At x=15%, full body of an anonymous tennis player, seen three quarters from behind in an elegant trophy pose, left arm high after the ball toss, racket cocked back in the right hand, both feet just behind the baseline, about 25% of frame height. Opponent deep near the far baseline. View from just behind and slightly to one side of the server. Deep petrol blues and a few warm rim highlights; textured acrylic court and faint audience bokeh.
